Step 5

The Cold Email

The most important step. Your email must be under 150 words — no exceptions. Busy professionals don't read long emails.

1

Subject Line 5–8 words

Decides if they open. Keep it short and specific.

2

Opening Hook 15–20 words

Show you've done your homework. Mention something specific about them or their company.

3

Your Credentials 40–50 words

Quick intro + 3 bullet points of your strongest achievements. Use numbers.

4

Value Proposition 30–40 words

What can you do for THEM? Don't just ask for a role — show value.

5

Call to Action 15–20 words

Ask for something small and specific. Make it easy to say yes.

6

Closing 10–15 words

Name, phone number, LinkedIn URL. That's it.

Subject Line Examples

Opportunity Inquiry – [Your College/Company] [Your Role/Major]
CAT 99%ile Candidate Seeking Consulting Role
[Alumni Name] Recommended I Reach Out
Can Help with [X Project] – [Your Name]

💡 Key Rules: Under 150 words total · Personalize the opening hook for each person · Use real numbers in achievements · One clear ask only

Action Plan

Your Weekly Schedule

Follow this every week without fail. Consistency is what separates people who land jobs from those who don't.

Monday

Research Day

  • Research 10 new companies
  • Find decision makers on LinkedIn
  • Add targets to tracker
Tuesday

Outreach Day 1

  • Send 15 personalized emails
  • Customize opening hook each time
  • Log all sends in tracker
Wednesday

Outreach Day 2

  • Send 15 personalized emails
  • Reply to any responses
  • Update statuses in tracker
Thursday

Outreach Day 3

  • Send 15 personalized emails
  • Check earlier responses
  • Schedule any calls booked
Friday

Follow-Up Day

  • Follow up on last week's no-replies
  • Wait 5–7 days before following up
  • Max 2 follow-ups per person
Weekend

Review & Plan

  • Update tracking sheet fully
  • Plan next week's targets
  • Note what's working / not
